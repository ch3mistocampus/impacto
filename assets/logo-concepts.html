Impacto — 10 logo concepts

Built from the app's existing palette (navy, gold, red, emerald) and type (Fraunces + Inter). Each card shows the full lockup plus a 56px app-icon tile. Toggle the background to check them on navy.

01Brick Stack

Three bricks in the three category colors, the top one still landing — every pledge adds to the build. Most literal to the donor flow.

02Roof A

The “a” becomes a roofline with a gold doorway. Reads as a word first, a home second — works well small as a standalone mark.

03Ripple

A nod to Espejo de Agua: one drop (a gift) spreading outward into verified impact. Calm, trustworthy, non-literal.

04Honest Ledger

Four bars = pledged → received → used → verified, the app's core promise that each stage is its own number. Data-forward, credible.

05Sprouting Brick

Reconstruction + “heal & sow” in one shape: life growing out of rebuilt ground. Warm and hopeful.

06i-House

A house silhouette holding a lowercase “i”, the dot as a gold light in the window. Strong, ownable app icon.

07Three Causes

Roof, brick, sprout — one glyph per category (roof & shelter, reconstruction, heal & sow). Explains the product at a glance.

08Verified Seal

An official-feeling stamp built around the check mark — leans into transparency and trust, matching the app's gold “seal fill”.

09Held Home

Two hands (donor + coordinator) cradling a home, with a gold sun rising. The most emotional, community-centered option.

10Progress Timeline

The final “o” is a target, underlined by the public timeline — pledge to verified, step by step. Modern product feel.
