## 1. Core product definition

Impacto is a **new, standalone transparency tool** for Espejo de Agua's recovery efforts. It connects:

1. **Field workers:** document damage and community needs with photos and voice in Spanish or English; create project drafts; add sensitive beneficiary details separately.
2. **Nonprofit coordinators:** review/approve projects, validate needs, manage donations, expenses, deliveries, and impact evidence.
3. **Donors:** browse approved material requests using a swipe-card interface, pledge specific quantities, and follow verified outcomes.
4. **Community members:** view accessible, privacy-safe recovery progress and feedback opportunities without exposure of personal or precise location data.

**Core narrative:** A need documented in the field becomes an approved project, then a concrete material request, then a pledge, delivery, and verified outcome visible on a public impact timeline.

## 4. MVP end-to-end flow — implement in this order

### A. Field worker creates a project using voice

- Start a **new field report** from mobile.
- Record audio in Spanish or English; allow typed text as fallback.
- Upload one or more photos of damage/needs.
- Transcribe voice, then use AI to propose **editable** fields:
  - public-safe project title and summary;
  - category (one of the three buckets);
  - damage/needs description;
  - suggested material line items with quantity and unit;
  - urgency **suggestion** and missing-information flags;
  - proposed milestones.
- Show the transcript and extraction side by side for confirmation.
- Never invent quantities or imply certainty when audio is ambiguous; use `needs_confirmation` and blank quantities.
- Field worker corrects details and saves as **draft**.
- **Separate restricted form:** household/beneficiary names, contact details, exact location/address, and other personal information. This form is optional during demo, access-controlled, and **never included in public cards or AI prompts by default**.
- Avoid recording GPS automatically. Strip image EXIF metadata and require review/redaction before any image is published.

**Example voice input (synthetic demo):**
> “Estamos ayudando a una familia cuya casa sufrió daños. Necesitamos aproximadamente 200 ladrillos, 15 bolsas de cemento y materiales para reparar el techo.”

Expected extraction: 200 bricks; 15 cement bags; roofing materials quantity **unconfirmed**. Private name/address entered separately.

### B. Nonprofit review and publication

- Coordinator sees pending reports and can edit/approve/reject/request changes.
- Approved report becomes a **project** with a public-safe title, category, needs, and milestones.
- Coordinator reviews all public photos and text before publication.
- Project states: `draft`, `pending_review`, `approved`, `active`, `paused_safety`, `completed`, `archived`.
- Store a reason for safety pauses; public version should be generalized.
- AI never approves, prioritizes beneficiaries, verifies deliveries, or certifies spending. Prioritization remains with nonprofit/community decision-makers.

### C. Swipe-to-donate material cards

- Donors swipe/browse **approved, published material needs**: e.g., “50 bricks,” “10 roofing sheets,” “5 cement bags,” “seed packets.”
- Card shows material, quantity remaining, project category, public-safe impact summary, and verified organization identity.
- Actions: **Pass**, **I have these materials**, **I can buy these**, **Pledge quantity**.
- Allow donor to select a quantity **up to the currently available unmet quantity**. Handle simultaneous pledges with a Convex mutation that rechecks remaining quantity atomically.
- No real payments required for MVP. Optional nonprofit-approved purchase/wishlist link; no invented checkout or unverified recipient account.
- Do not reveal beneficiary name, household location, or raw field photos.
- Make clear that pledging is **not** delivering or funding a project.

### D. Verification and evidence

Track material quantities through distinct states:

`requested → pledged → received → delivered → used → verified`

- Pledge: donor commitment only.
- Received: nonprofit physically receives materials.
- Delivered: materials reach approved project (verified by authorized staff).
- Used: materials installed or consumed.
- Verified: nonprofit confirms milestone/evidence.

Support evidence attachment: receipt, delivery photo, signed handover document, and staff notes. Keep **original evidence private**; only approved/redacted evidence becomes public. Record who changed each state and when. Do not count pledges as delivered impact.

### E. Public transparency dashboard

Show three program buckets and projects within each. For each project, show:

- Original public-safe need and approval date.
- Materials **requested / pledged / received / delivered / used / verified**.
- Donations **received / allocated / spent** as separate financial measures, if financial entries exist.
- Evidence-backed milestones and a chronological **Impact Timeline**.
- Privacy-reviewed before/after images (optional).
- Plain-language Spanish/English explanation of **how assistance changed the situation**.
- Safety-paused status where applicable.
- Clearly labeled **as-of date**, evidence status, and demo/verified distinction.

For expenses: store original currency (`COP`, `USD`, etc.), exact recorded amount, receipt status, and any actual conversion details. Do not manufacture FX conversions or equate a pledge to cash received.

## 5. Suggested Convex schema extensions

Adapt to existing schema instead of blindly recreating tables.

| Table / entity | Essential fields |
|---|---|
| `users` | role, language, nonprofitId, access flags |
| `fieldReports` | reporterId, originalLanguage, transcript, extractedDraft, photos, status, timestamps |
| `beneficiariesPrivate` | reportId/projectId, name, contact, exactLocation, restrictedNotes; private access only |
| `projects` | titleEs/titleEn, category, publicSummaryEs/publicSummaryEn, status, approval metadata, publishedAt |
| `materialNeeds` | projectId, item, unit, quantityRequested, publicationStatus |
| `materialPledges` | needId, donorId, quantity, pledgeStatus, timestamps |
| `materialEvents` | needId, pledgeId?, stage, quantity, actorId, evidenceId?, timestamp |
| `contributions` | projectId?, donorId?, amount, currency, status, type, sourceReference |
| `expenses` | projectId, amount, currency, supplierRegion?, laborFlag?, receiptId?, approvalStatus |
| `evidence` | projectId, privateFileId, publicApprovedFileId?, type, reviewedBy?, reviewStatus |
| `milestones` | projectId, description, status, verifiedBy?, verifiedAt? |
| `impactMetrics` | projectId, metricKey, unit, value, verificationStatus, date |
| `weeklyReports` | periodStart, periodEnd, snapshot, reviewedBy, publishedAt |
| `auditEvents` | actorId, entityType, entityId, action, timestamp, summary |

**Implementation requirements:** server-enforced role checks in every sensitive Convex query/mutation/action; no public query of private beneficiary or unreviewed evidence; avoid trusting client-supplied roles; limit AI prompts to non-sensitive data. Derive material totals from verified events/pledges or enforce consistent atomic aggregates. Maintain a visible audit trail for financial and verification changes.

## 6. Nonprofit-specific outcome metrics (post-MVP unless easy)

Make metrics configurable per recovery bucket. Examples:

- **Roof and Shelter:** households supported, square meters of roofing installed, emergency shelter deliveries.
- **Reconstruction:** houses repaired/completed, materials delivered/used, local labor payments, share of regional procurement.
- **Heal and Sow:** gatherings held, approximate participation per gathering (a headcount only, never who attended), and a short report of how each one went.
- **Transparency:** weekly reports published, donation receipts recorded, delivery records signed, milestones verified, outstanding unverified claims.

Never claim a household was helped solely because materials were pledged. Use clear metric definitions and evidence requirements. Public views should use aggregates and privacy-safe summaries, especially for small communities.

Use large touch targets, low-bandwidth image compression, clear status labels, and accessible contrast. Typed intake should work if microphone access or AI service fails. Avoid promising true offline sync unless actually implemented.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

- A Spanish voice note can produce an editable material request without leaking private details.
- English text and voice work with the same structured workflow.
- Unapproved reports and materials do not appear in donor cards.
- A 50-brick pledge changes pledged quantity, **not delivered quantity**.
- Staff-only confirmation advances received/delivered/verified counts.
- A donor can see a project impact timeline without household name or precise location.
- A safety pause is visible as a legitimate state, not mislabeled as failure.
- Each public metric can be traced to an authorized record and appropriate evidence.
- No real beneficiaries, financial transactions, or deliveries are fabricated for the demo.
