Cinco direcciones para Impacto, desde Espejo de Agua y Colombia

A second round, quieter than the first. Each direction starts from something real: Espejo de Agua's own palette and type, the bahareque towns of Quindío, the flag, the coffee landscape and the wax palms of Cocora. Every mock uses the same need, 200 bricks with 80 pledged, 50 received and delivered, 40 used and 30 verified, so the comparison is fair.

Type

Lora 400 for headlines and figures, Karla 400–600 for interface text. Both are the nonprofit's fonts.

Character and risk

Instantly feels like Espejo de Agua, so trust carries over. The risk is that a single-hue palette makes the stages harder to tell apart, so the ledger leans on line style (dashed promise, thicker verified) as well as color.

Bahareque

The towns of the Eje Cafetero, Pijao included: lime-washed walls, painted wooden balconies and doors in deep blue, ochre and oxblood. The colors appear the way they do on a street, as painted trim on white, never as a background. Doorway arches frame the materials, and a painted zócalo closes each surface.

Type

Libre Caslon Text for headlines and figures. It has the feel of a town notice board. Karla for interface text.

Character and risk

Rooted in the place without being folkloric, and the most distinctive of the five. The arches and zócalo need restraint: use them once per screen at most.

Tricolor

The Colombian flag, used with the restraint of an official document. Yellow, blue and red appear only in the flag's own 2:1:1 proportions, as a fine band at the top of a page or card. Everything else is white, navy and Bodoni, set with the composure of a certificate. Made for institutional donors and the weekly public report.

Palma de cera

The wax palm, Colombia's national tree, rising out of the cloud forest of Cocora in Quindío. Mist greys, pale sky and a slender vertical rhythm. The ledger is drawn as six stems whose height is the quantity, and only verified stems get a crown. The lightest and most contemplative direction.

Character and risk

The most elegant and calm of the five. The light type and low contrast need care: keep body text at full ink and test the stem chart with screen readers, using a table as the accessible version.

My recommendation: Agua quieta as the system, with Bahareque's doors for the materials

Agua quieta is the one I'd build. Donors arriving from espejodeagua.org will recognize it immediately, it uses the nonprofit's own fonts, and its calm suits a recovery project better than anything louder. Its weakness is a single-hue palette, and Bahareque fixes that: borrow the arched door frame for material cards and the ochre and balcony blue for the stage colors.

Keep across every direction

Six ledger stages drawn separately, never stacked. Promises are dashed. Only verified gets the strongest mark.

Programs by shape, not color

Techo is a triangle, Reconstrucción a square, Sanar y sembrar a circle, so green can mean verified and nothing else.

Tricolor's later role

Tricolor's certificate layout would be a good fit for the weekly report and donor certificates, even if it's not the main style.
